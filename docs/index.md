# FUM AI Archive

Welcome to the **FUM AI Archive** — a public, long-term archive of educational
resources, repositories, and work produced by the teaching assistants of the
AI course.

!!! warning "This is not the official course website"
    This site is an independent archive maintained by former and current
    teaching assistants. It does **not** replace the university Moodle. All
    official announcements, deadlines, submissions, and grading are managed
    exclusively through Moodle. If anything on this archive ever conflicts
    with Moodle, **Moodle is authoritative**.

## What is FUM-AI-Archive?

FUM-AI-Archive is a GitHub organization that collects and preserves the
materials, code, and outcomes generated across semesters of the AI course.
Instead of letting valuable teaching material disappear once a semester
ends, this archive keeps it organized, searchable, and accessible for
students, teaching assistants, and instructors — now and in the future.

## Purpose

The goals of this archive are to:

- Preserve course materials beyond the lifetime of any single semester.
- Give future teaching assistants a reference point for how the course has
  evolved.
- Provide students with a browsable history of assignments and projects.
- Centralize links to the repositories produced by each semester's cohort.

## An archive, not a course platform

Everything on this site is historical or reference material. Semesters are
added here only after they are underway or completed. This site does not
handle submissions, grading, announcements, or live communication — that is
Moodle's job.

## How to navigate this archive

- **[About](about.md)** — more detail on the organization and how it is
  maintained.
- **[Repositories](repositories.md)** — a directory of active repositories
  under the FUM-AI-Archive GitHub organization.
- **Archive** — the main body of the site, organized by semester
  (e.g. *Fall 2025*, *Spring 2026*). Each semester section is
  self-contained and includes its team, schedule, assignments, projects and resources.
- **[Contributing](contributing.md)** — how teaching assistants can add or
  update content, including instructions for archiving a new semester.

Use the search bar at the top of the page or the sidebar navigation to find
what you're looking for.
