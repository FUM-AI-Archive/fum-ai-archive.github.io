# About

Welcome to the **FUM AI Archive** — a public, long-term archive of educational
resources, repositories, and work produced by the teaching assistants of the
AI course.

!!! warning "This is not the official course website"
    This site is an independent archive maintained by former and current
    teaching assistants. It does **not** replace the university Moodle. All
    official announcements, deadlines, submissions, and grading are managed
    exclusively through Moodle. If anything on this archive ever conflicts
    with Moodle, **Moodle is authoritative**.

## What is FUM-AI-Archive?

FUM-AI-Archive is a GitHub organization created and maintained by teaching
assistants of the AI course. It collects and preserves the materials, code,
and outcomes generated across semesters of the course, so that valuable
teaching material doesn't disappear once a semester ends. Its purpose is to
serve as a durable, public record of the course's materials and the work
produced around it, separate from any single semester or any single
person's involvement.

This website is the documentation front-end for that organization, built
with [MkDocs](https://www.mkdocs.org/) and the
[Material for MkDocs](https://squidfunk.github.io/mkdocs-material/) theme.

## Purpose

Course materials tend to live in scattered places: a Moodle course that gets
reset or archived, individual student repositories, chat logs, and personal
drives. Once a semester ends, this material is easy to lose track of. The
FUM-AI-Archive organization exists to counter that by:

- Preserving course materials beyond the lifetime of any single semester.
- Hosting a stable set of repositories per semester, with a consistent
  documentation structure across semesters.
- Giving future teaching assistants a reference point for how the course
  has evolved.
- Providing students with a browsable history of assignments and projects.
- Centralizing links to the repositories produced by each semester's cohort.

## Relationship to the university course

This project is **not**:

- The official course website.
- A replacement for Moodle.
- A source of official announcements, deadlines, or grading information.

This project **is**:

- A community-maintained archive built by teaching assistants.
- A reference resource for students and future teaching staff.
- A historical record of how the course has evolved over time.

Everything on this site is historical or reference material. Semesters are
added here only after they are underway or completed. This site does not
handle submissions, grading, announcements, or live communication — that is
Moodle's job.

## How to navigate this archive

- **Archive** — the main body of the site, organized by semester
  (e.g. *Fall 2025*, *Spring 2026*). Each semester section is
  self-contained and includes its team, schedule, assignments, projects and
  resources. Repositories produced during a semester are linked from that
  semester's own Repositories page.

Use the search bar at the top of the page or the sidebar navigation to find
what you're looking for.

## Maintainers

This site and its associated repositories are maintained collaboratively by
current and former teaching assistants of the AI course. To get involved,
open an issue or pull request on the
[FUM-AI-Archive](https://github.com/FUM-AI-Archive) GitHub organization.
