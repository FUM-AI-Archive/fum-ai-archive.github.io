# Fall 2025

Welcome to the archive of the **Artificial Intelligence** course offered during the **Fall 2025** semester at **Ferdowsi University of Mashhad**.

This section documents the organization of the course, educational materials prepared by the teaching team, repositories, assignments, projects, and other resources developed throughout the semester.

> **Note**
>
> This archive is provided for documentation and reference purposes only. During the active semester, all announcements, submissions, and grading were managed through the university VU.

---

## Course Assessment

The tutorial component of the course accounted for **12 points** of the overall course grade and consisted of the following components.

| Component | Weight |
|-----------|-------:|
| Theory Assignments (8 × 0.5) | 4.0 |
| Midterm I | 2.0 |
| Midterm II | 2.0 |
| Machine Learning Quiz I | 0.5 |
| Machine Learning Quiz II | 0.5 |
| Programming Projects | 3.0 |
| **Total** | **12.0** |

### Theory Assignments

Eight handwritten theory assignments were released throughout the semester.

Each assignment carried **0.5 points** and remained available for **one week**.

Assignments were discussed and solved during the following tutorial session. For this reason, official written solutions were **not** published.

Students who actively participated during the tutorial session could receive up to **10% bonus** on the corresponding assignment. This bonus was capped by the assignment's original score and could not increase the assignment beyond its maximum grade.

For the list of assignments and their topics, see the **Assignments** page.

### Midterm Examinations

To distribute the course workload more evenly, two midterm examinations were held.

- **Midterm I (2 points):** Covered the first four theory assignments.
- **Midterm II (2 points):** Covered the remaining four theory assignments.

### Machine Learning Quizzes

Two short quizzes were held during the semester.

| Quiz | Topic | Weight |
|------|-------|-------:|
| Quiz I | Linear Regression | 0.5 |
| Quiz II | Decision Trees | 0.5 |

All quizzes were announced in advance and conducted during tutorial sessions.

### Programming Projects

Programming projects accounted for **3 points** of the tutorial grade.

| Project | Weight |
|---------|-------:|
| Reinforcement Learning | 1.50 |
| Constraint Satisfaction Problems | 0.75 |
| Regression & Genetic Algorithms | 0.75 |
