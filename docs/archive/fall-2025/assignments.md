# Theory Assignments — Fall 2025

This page archives the handwritten theory assignments prepared for the
**Artificial Intelligence** course during the **Fall 2025** semester.

> This page is intended for archival purposes only.
> Assignment submissions, grading, and announcements were managed through
> the university VU.

---

## LaTeX Assignment Template

All handwritten assignments were prepared using a shared LaTeX template
developed and maintained by the teaching team to provide a consistent
layout and appearance throughout the semester.

The template is publicly available in the course repository:

- **LaTeX Template:**  
  https://github.com/FUM-AI-Archive/ai-assignments-2025-fall/tree/main/latex-template
